🧮 Mathematical Model

1. Average Petrol Price

Average = Sum of recorded petrol prices ÷ Number of cities

2. Petrol-Diesel Price Gap

Price Gap = Petrol Price − Diesel Price

3. Estimated Fuel Required

Fuel Required (litres) = Distance (km) ÷ Mileage (km/L)

4. Estimated Trip Cost

Trip Cost = Fuel Required × Fuel Price per litre

5. Percentage Price Change

New Price = Original Price × (1 + Percentage Change ÷ 100)

6. E20 Composition

Ethanol Volume = Total Volume × 20 ÷ 100
Petrol Volume = Total Volume × 80 ÷ 100

Computational Thinking Used

  • Data collection: Record city prices, date and source.
  • Decomposition: Divide the problem into data, comparison and calculation.
  • Pattern recognition: Find higher and lower recorded prices.
  • Abstraction: Focus on relevant data instead of unrelated details.
  • Algorithm: Apply the same formulas to every entry.
  • Validation: Check that values are valid and sources are recorded.

The model estimates costs. Actual mileage, traffic, driving conditions and fuel prices may differ.

🎓 Viva Questions and Answers

Q1. What is the purpose of this project?

To use mathematical models and computational thinking to compare fuel-price data and estimate travel expenses.

Q2. Why is data collection important?

Accurate data makes the comparison more meaningful. Each entry should include a date and a reliable source.

Q3. What is an average?

The sum of all values divided by the number of values.

Q4. What is computational thinking?

A problem-solving approach that uses decomposition, pattern recognition, abstraction and algorithms.

Q5. What is E20?

E20 is a fuel blend containing 20% ethanol and 80% petrol by volume.

Q6. Does this website show live fuel prices?

No. It analyses the data entered by the user. The website does not connect to a live price service.

Q7. How is the trip cost calculated?

Distance is divided by mileage to estimate fuel required, then multiplied by the price per litre.

Q8. What makes this project useful?

Users can analyse their own researched data, compare cities, estimate journey costs and test hypothetical price changes instead of viewing only static information.
